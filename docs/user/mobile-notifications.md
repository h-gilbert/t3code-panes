# Mobile notifications

T3 Code sends push alerts for approvals, requests for input, and failures. Completion
alerts are off, including for turns submitted from your phone.

A self-hosted iPhone app can receive push alerts directly from a paired environment.
Allow notifications in iOS Settings, then open the app and connect to the environment
to register the phone.

The T3 Connect app uses your account to receive alerts in the background. On Android,
notifications require Android 7.0 or newer and Google Play services. Manage app
permissions and the **Agent alerts** channel in Android Settings. If you force-stop
T3 Code, open it again to resume delivery.

Alerts stay quiet while the mobile app is in the foreground. Viewing a thread on
another device does not silence your phone's alerts.
